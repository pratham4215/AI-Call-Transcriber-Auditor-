Brio scoring: reading ratings by probability

Switch: AUDIT_SCORING=brio
Before
The model writes the whole answer

~140
steps for one stock call · Brio needs one per rating
Every chip is one model step, including all the brackets, quotes and names.
The stock call wrote about 140 tokens at about 10 per second; that writing was 72% of its time.
Brio
The model only answers: which option?

Code writes the JSON; the model takes one step per rating, and the most likely option is picked.
Each pick sees the earlier picks, like normal writing. Picks under 70% sure are flagged "Check". Percentages above are an example.
Fine-tuned model
2.6× faster
545 test calls: same accuracy (81.5% both ways), 97.7% identical ratings. 13.5 s → 5.2 s per call.
Stock model
~49% less time
40 real audits: 96.5% identical evidence states. 18.8 s → about 9.6 s per call (estimate).
Checked against humans
5 of 7
Where Brio and normal writing disagreed, human auditors sided with Brio 5 times out of 7.
Safety net
Falls back
Yes/no answers and option lists are picked too; only turn IDs and short reasons are written. Any Brio error: that call is written normally.
